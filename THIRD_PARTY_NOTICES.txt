packages/api/gateway/src/stream-server.ts, DeepSeek Harness.
Copyright (c) 2026 DeepSeek. MIT License (full text above).
Uses the public Gateway wireStream transport and public stream-protocol parser.
Local changes also bound pending downlink bytes and stalled writes, and release
cancelled stream ownership without waiting for unresponsive provider iterators.
